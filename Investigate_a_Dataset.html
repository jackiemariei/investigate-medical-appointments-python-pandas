Introduction¶

Dataset Description¶

This dataset contains information about medical appointments in Brazil and whether patients showed up for their scheduled apointments. It includes over 100,000 records and provides details such as patient age, gender, medical condition and if they received a text reminder. Some of the key variables in the dataset include age, gender, hypertension, diabetes, scholarship status, and SMS reminders. The main outcome variable indicates whether a patient missed their appointment. The purpose of this analysis is to explore patters in the data and identify factors that may be associated with patients not showing up for their appointments.

Question(s) for Analysis¶

  1. What is the distribution of patient age in the dataset?
  2. How does receiving an SMS reminder relate to appointment no-show rates?
  3. How do SMS reminders and patient age relate to apointment no-show rates?

Conclusions¶

This analysis explored factors that may influence whether patients miss their medical appointments. Variables such as SMS reminders, hipertension, financial assistance, and age were examined in relation to no-show rates.

The results showed that patients who received SMS reminders had a higher no-show rate compared to those who did not receive reminders. Patients with hipertension were less likely to miss appointments, while patients receiving financial assistance had slightly higher no-show rates. Additionally, younger patients appeared more likely to miss appointments compared to older patients.

These findings are based on observational data and do not imply causation. There may be other factors not included in this dataset, such as transportation, work schedules, or personal circumstances, that influence whether a patient attends their appoitnment.

A limiations of this analysis is that it only examines a limited number of variables and does not account for potential confounding factors. Future analysis could include additional variables to better understand causes of missed appointments.
